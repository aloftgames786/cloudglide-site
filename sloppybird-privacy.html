Privacy Policy

SloppyBird · Last updated: July 2026

Overview

SloppyBird is developed by Aloft Games. This privacy policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights as a user. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

SloppyBird does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or contact details. Your game progress (scores, stars, upgrades, and settings) is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences.

SloppyBird uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): to show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): anonymous usage and event data (such as game sessions and game-over events) is collected to help us understand how the game is used and improve it. This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional): if you choose to sign in to submit scores to the leaderboard, your Google Play Games profile is used to record and display your ranking. Signing in is optional.

In-App Purchases

SloppyBird does not offer in-app purchases. All in-game upgrades are earned by playing.

Third-Party Services

SloppyBird uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase / Analytics — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services — policies.google.com/privacy

Your Choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads. You can also play without signing in to Google Play Games.

Children's Privacy

SloppyBird is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
